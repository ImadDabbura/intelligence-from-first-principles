# Attribution and source material

The MLST collection reviews [Machine Learning Street Talk](https://www.youtube.com/@MachineLearningStreetTalk/videos), produced by its creators and guests. This is an independent set of research notes, not an official MLST publication or endorsement.

I used publisher descriptions, podcast notes, references and available transcripts to assemble the collection. AI agents assisted with screening, transcript review and reconciliation. The reviews preserve the distinction between the episode’s claims and the interpretation or criticism in my notes.

The public repository includes original episode titles and links, screening decisions, analytical notes, resource links, coverage caveats and hashes of the transcript files used. It does not distribute downloaded transcripts, recordings, full publisher descriptions or raw retrieval archives. Those remain in my private working collection.

The hashes identify the reviewed files; they do not make the transcript review independently reproducible without access to the corresponding source texts. Public source links and edition notes are provided so readers can inspect the original material. Resource links are preserved as publisher references, not endorsements or a claim that every resource was checked.

Rights in source recordings, descriptions, transcripts and referenced papers remain with their respective holders. No third-party material is relicensed by inclusion of a title, reference or link here. This repository does not currently grant a general reuse license for its notes or code.
