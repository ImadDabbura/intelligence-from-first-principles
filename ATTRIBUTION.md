# Attribution and source material

The Library lists talks, lectures, interviews and papers produced by their creators, speakers and authors, starting with [Machine Learning Street Talk](https://www.youtube.com/@MachineLearningStreetTalk/videos). This is an independent set of research notes, not an official publication or endorsement of any of them.

I used publisher descriptions, podcast notes and references to assemble the collection. AI agents assisted with screening and with tying items to core ideas.

The public repository includes original titles and links, screening decisions, short reasons, resource links and coverage caveats. It does not distribute downloaded transcripts, recordings, full publisher descriptions or raw retrieval archives. Those remain in my private working collection. Resource links are preserved as publisher references, not endorsements or a claim that every resource was checked.

Rights in source recordings, descriptions, transcripts and referenced papers remain with their respective holders. No third-party material is relicensed by inclusion of a title, reference or link here. This repository does not currently grant a general reuse license for its notes or code.
