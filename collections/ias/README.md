# Institute for Advanced Study

## Why I am studying this

Originator-grade expositions of unifying programmes of learning and intelligence: the person who built the idea derives what follows from it. The bar comes from three exemplars: Yi Ma on rate reduction and Friston/Parr on the free-energy principle (both MLST), and Naftali Tishby's Stanford seminar on the information theory of deep learning.
