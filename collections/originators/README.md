# Originators by school

## Why I am studying this

To learn the root principles (uṣūl) of every school of thought on learning and intelligence from each school's own primary sources, then weigh them by proof. Each entry is an originator's or a leading carrier's own exposition of their programme.

## Scope

- Method: people-first search, two passes (founders, then second-generation figures and seven further schools), 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-22.
- Coverage: 27 schools, 185 people, 492 items, all high priority.
- Formats: lectures, tutorials, long interviews and the originators' own expository writing. Textbooks excluded.
- Known gaps: many videos were verified by title and channel only after YouTube blocked detailed lookups; some people have writing only (no verifiable recording); some archival recordings are unofficial uploads. Each caveat says what was verified.
- Number reviewed in depth: 0
