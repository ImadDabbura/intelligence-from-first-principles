# Originators by school

## Why I am studying this

To learn the root principles (uṣūl) of every school of thought on learning and intelligence from each school's own primary sources, then weigh them by proof. Each entry is an originator's own exposition of their programme.

## Scope

- Method: people-first search across 20 schools and 89 originators, on 2026-11-21.
- Formats: lectures, tutorials, long interviews and the originators' own expository writing.
- Kept here: 224 items, all high priority.
- Known gaps: about 40 videos were verified by title and channel only (YouTube blocked detailed lookups); Pezzulo, Vitányi and Mehta have writing only. Second-generation figures and further schools are a planned second pass.
- Number reviewed in depth: 0
