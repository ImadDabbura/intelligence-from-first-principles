# Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing

## Why I am studying this

Simons Institute programmes gather the originators of learning theory, statistical physics of learning, causality and computational neuroscience in tutorials and workshops. This collection keeps only the talks that pass the originator bar: the person who built a programme derives what follows from it.

## Scope

- Original source: https://www.youtube.com/@SimonsInstituteTOC
- Inventory date: 2026-11-21
- Included formats: Lecture (workshop talks, boot-camp tutorials, open lectures)
- Selection: 57 of 287 playlists relevant to learning, intelligence, the brain, information and statistical physics.
- Number screened: 1,261 talks: 42 high, 453 medium, 742 skip, 24 insufficient information.
- Kept here: the 42 high-priority talks only.
- Known gaps: course-type playlists (such as the 2022 Deep Learning Theory summer school) could not be listed; 39 videos were private or deleted; many older talks have no abstract, so their screening rests on title and speaker (see each caveat).
- Number reviewed in depth: 0
